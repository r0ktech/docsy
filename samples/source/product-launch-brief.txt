Product Launch Brief: Tidewater Notes 3.0

Summary
Tidewater Notes 3.0 introduces offline sync, shared notebooks, and a redesigned editor. The public launch is planned for the second week of May, after a four-week private beta with 400 invited users.

Goals
- Reach 25,000 weekly active users within 60 days of launch.
- Convert 4 percent of free users to the paid plan within the first quarter.
- Keep the crash-free session rate above 99.5 percent.

Audience
The primary audience is students and independent researchers who take long-form notes. The secondary audience is small teams of up to ten people who need shared notebooks.

Pricing
The free plan keeps unlimited personal notes. The paid plan costs 6 USD per month or 60 USD per year and adds shared notebooks, version history, and offline sync on unlimited devices.

Risks
The largest risk is sync conflicts when two people edit the same note offline. The engineering team will ship a conflict viewer that shows both versions side by side. A second risk is that the redesigned editor may confuse existing users, so the old editor will remain available for 90 days behind a settings toggle.

Launch checklist owner
The launch checklist is owned by the product marketing lead, who will run a go or no-go review three days before launch.

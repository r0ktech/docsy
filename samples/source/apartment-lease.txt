RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into between Maple Row Properties LLC ("Landlord") and the tenant named in the schedule ("Tenant") for the apartment located at Unit 4B, 18 Alder Street ("Premises").

1. NOTICES
All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered by email or by first-class post to the addresses listed in the schedule. Notices sent by email are effective on the next business day.

2. TERM
The Lease begins on the commencement date in the schedule and continues for a fixed term of twelve (12) months ("Term").

3. RENT
Tenant shall pay monthly rent of 1,850 USD, due on the first day of each month. Rent may be paid by bank transfer to the account designated by Landlord. If rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, a late fee of 75 USD will be charged.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to one month's rent before move-in. Landlord will hold the deposit in a separate account and return it, minus any lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear, within thirty (30) days after Tenant vacates the Premises.

5. UTILITIES
Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet, and gas. Landlord is responsible for water, sewer, and trash collection.

6. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used only as a private residence. Tenant shall not run a business that brings customers to the Premises. No more than two adults may reside in the Premises without Landlord's written consent.

7. PETS
Tenant may keep one cat or one dog under 25 pounds with Landlord's prior written approval. A non-refundable pet fee of 300 USD applies. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the pet.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and promptly report any needed repairs. Landlord shall make repairs to plumbing, heating, and electrical systems within a reasonable time, and within 24 hours for emergencies such as loss of heat in winter or major leaks.

9. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord may enter the Premises for inspections, repairs, or showings after giving Tenant at least 24 hours' notice, except in an emergency.

10. ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall not paint, install fixtures, or make other alterations without Landlord's written consent. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are permitted.

11. INSURANCE
Tenant is strongly encouraged to carry renter's insurance. Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's personal property.

12. TERMINATION AND RENEWAL
12.1 This Lease shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis unless either party provides at least sixty (60) days' written notice prior to the end of the Term of its intention not to renew.
12.2 During a month-to-month renewal, either party may end the tenancy with thirty (30) days' written notice.
12.3 Tenant shall return all keys and access devices to Landlord on or before the final day of occupancy.
12.4 Any property remaining after vacating may be disposed of in accordance with applicable law.

13. EARLY TERMINATION
Tenant may end the Lease before the end of the Term by giving sixty (60) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to one month's rent.

14. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises are located.

15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Lease, together with the schedule, is the entire agreement between the parties and replaces any prior discussions.
